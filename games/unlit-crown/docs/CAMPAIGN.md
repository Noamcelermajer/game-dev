# The Unlit Crown: Campaign Plan

Target: **about 6.5 hours on the main path**, 9 to 10 hours with side content. Built from a prologue, 7 chapters, 1 interlude and a finale, with a Warden boss closing each chapter.

Rule of thumb for each chapter: **10 min** to arrive and explore, **20 min** on the main quest chain, **5 min** on a mid-chapter set piece, **10 min** for the boss (including retries), and **15–25 min** of optional side quests.

## Systems that span the campaign

### The Lantern Caravan (hub)
A moving camp that travels to the edge of each new region. It is the save point, shop, crafting bench, talent respec and, later, the **co-op lobby**. NPCs you rescue join it, so the camp visibly grows as you play.

### Three hidden choice meters
Major choices push one of three meters. Their totals decide which endings unlock.

| Meter | Raised by |
| --- | --- |
| **Mercy** | sparing, freeing and healing; refusing to use people |
| **Order** | keeping shards, upholding law, siding with Crown institutions |
| **Memory** | collecting Hollow memories, listening to the dead, preserving the past |

### Crown shards
Each Warden drops a shard. You can **claim** it (a strong passive bonus that raises Order and corrupts slightly) or **give it to the Kindled forge** (a weaker bonus that raises Mercy). This is the main recurring choice.

### Hollow Memories
3 hidden collectibles per region: short playable flashbacks of 30–60 seconds that show the past from the Hollow's side. All 24 unlock the true version of the Hollow ending.

## Main cast

| Character | Role | Arc |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **Wren Ashby** | Kindled smuggler, mentor | Once served the Regent as a lantern-breaker. Confesses this in Chapter 6 and can die there. |
| **Nim** | A girl with no memory, found in the prologue | The last memory of Orrin's daylight, given a body by the Hollow. Revealed in Chapter 7. |

| **Lady Seraphine** | The Regent's daughter | Secretly leads the dark-district rebels in Solace. |

## Prologue: Lanternfall (Ashgrove) · 20 min · Lv 1–3

**Hook:** At the harvest festival, the village's crown-lit lantern tower goes out for the first time in 300 years. The Hollow pours in.

**Beats**
1. Festival tutorial: movement, talking to villagers, the light radius.
2. The tower dies. Your hand ignites with a flame nobody gave you. **Class selection happens here**, framed as which way your flame wants to burn.
3. Escort villagers to the chapel through the dark (teaches that light keeps enemies back).
4. Inside the dead lantern tower you find **Nim**, asleep and unharmed.
5. **Mini-boss: the Lantern-Eater**, a Hollow beast feeding on the tower. Teaches dodge-roll and attack patterns.
6. Crown inquisitors arrive and brand you a heretic. **Wren** smuggles you and Nim out.

**Mechanics introduced:** movement, basic combat, light radius, Dread, dialogue.
**Ending:** the Lantern Caravan is founded with 3 NPCs.

## Chapter 1: The Candlewife (Waxmere) · 45 min · Lv 3–7

**Hook:** Wren needs supplies from Waxmere, a city famous for sheltering orphans. No child has left it in 40 years.

**Beats**
1. Arrive by boat across black water. The city is beautiful, warm and wrong.
2. The townsfolk adore "Mother". Investigate the missing children.
3. **Set piece:** the wax catacombs. Your light melts walls to open paths, but melting too much floods rooms.
4. Find **Pell** in a wax shell, still alive. Free him (Mercy) or leave him sealed for later so you don't alert Ysolde (no change).
5. Learn the truth: Ysolde lost her own children to the Hollow and now preserves every child she can.

**Boss: Mother Ysolde**
- Phase 1: she shields herself in wax; your light melts it.
- Phase 2: sealed children wake as wax husks. You can kill them or step back and let them return to sleep (Mercy).
- Phase 3: Ysolde begs you to stop. Finish her, or let her keep one candle and live (Mercy +, she joins the caravan as a healer).

**Shard of Keeping:** claim it (bonus to max HP) or forge it (heals on lantern use).
**Lore reveal:** the Wardens were chosen for their virtues, and the shards turn virtues into obsessions.
**Side quests:** *Ferryman's Debt* (smuggling job), *Unlit Choir Practice* (find 3 lost candles; reward: a Wickpriest trinket).

---

## Chapter 2: The Sleepless (Bellhold) · 45 min · Lv 7–11

**Hook:** The road to the north passes Bellhold, where the bell has rung every hour for 300 years and no one sleeps.

**Beats**
1. The fortress is full of soldiers who are mad from exhaustion. You need a pass to cross.
2. Meet **Captain Odile Varr**, who secretly sleeps and is plotting to stop the bell.
3. **Set piece:** each bell toll is a timed blackout. Stealth sections through the fortress between tolls.
4. Nim falls asleep at the camp and dreams real memories of daylight. The first hint about her.
5. Discover why Halvard keeps everyone awake: dreams really do open doors to the Hollow, and he isn't entirely wrong.

**Boss: Sir Halvard**
- Phase 1: duel in the bell tower; his bell-mace rings and stuns.
- Phase 2: the bell tolls and the arena goes dark. He hunts by sound, so standing still hides you.
- Phase 3: break the bell (Mercy, Halvard sleeps and dies peacefully) or keep it whole (Order, the fortress stays an anti-Hollow bastion for later).

**Choice:** Odile defects to the caravan if the bell breaks; otherwise she stays and holds Bellhold.
**Side quests:** *Ten Minutes of Sleep* (help 5 soldiers nap without being caught), *The Dream Courier* (letters delivered through dreams).

---

## Interlude: Cinderwell · 15 min · Lv 11

**Hook:** The caravan reaches Cinderwell, the hidden base of the Kindled Underground.

**Beats**
1. Meet other Kindled. Your flame is the strongest any of them have seen.
2. The faction choice: pledge to the **Kindled** (forge discounts, rebel quests), stay neutral, or quietly inform the **Crown** (gold, Order +).
3. The Kindled forge opens: shards given here become shared upgrades.
4. Wren explains the Wardens' map and points you to the Inkwell Archive, the only full map of Vael.

**Purpose:** pacing breather, a commerce and crafting tutorial, and the first big faction choice.

---

## Chapter 3: The Cartographer (Inkwell Archive) · 45 min · Lv 11–15

**Hook:** Ines Marrow has the only map of where the remaining shards are, and she is erasing whole regions of the world from it.

**Beats**
1. A library labyrinth whose layout changes each time you leave a room.
2. **Set piece:** erased zones are blank white areas where enemies and walls don't exist until you light them.
3. Find Ines's journal: her brother **Tobin** walked into the Hollow to map it and never came back. She is erasing the Hollow so nobody else is lost.
4. Copy the true map with the archive's living quill (puzzle).
5. **Specialisation unlocks at level 15** at the end of the chapter.

**Boss: Ines Marrow**
- Phase 1: she draws walls and traps in real time on the floor.
- Phase 2: she erases parts of the arena, including the floor under you.
- Phase 3: shine your light on the blank areas to restore them. Final choice: tell her Tobin may still be alive (Memory +) or let her die believing she failed.

**Lore reveal:** the map shows the shards' locations, and the crown's original position: Orrin's body is not in the sky but under Solace.
**Side quests:** *Overdue* (return 7 cursed books), *The Margin Notes* (a scholar's hidden notes reveal Wren's past).

---

## Chapter 4: The Loyal Hound (Ashen Steppe) · 40 min · Lv 15–19

**Hook:** The map leads across the Ashen Steppe to a shard guarded by Brannoc, a knight who was turned into a hound and has guarded his dead lord's grave for 300 years.

**Beats**
1. An open-world wasteland region with ash storms that shrink your light radius.
2. Mounted travel unlocked (a lantern-ox).
3. Locals tell conflicting legends of Brannoc: monster, saint, bandit.
4. **Set piece:** a stampede through a firestorm escaping ember vultures.
5. Find the lord's diary: Brannoc's lord asked him to "guard what matters". Brannoc chose the grave.

**Boss: Brannoc (with a peaceful option)**
- Combat route: 3 phases of fire, charges and howls that summon ash hyenas.
- **Peaceful route:** carry the grave lantern away from him without fighting. He follows you, and you lead him to the lord's real burial place. He lies down and lets go.
- Peaceful completion gives Mercy ++ and a unique companion pet, an ember pup.

**Purpose:** the emotional midpoint, and proof that not every boss has to die.
**Side quests:** *The Ash Wedding* (a marriage in a ruined chapel), *Herd Master* (round up stray lantern-oxen).

---

## Chapter 5: The Choir (Saint's Hollow) · 45 min · Lv 19–24

**Hook:** The monks of Saint's Hollow hold a shard and have begun to sing as one. Pilgrims who enter join the song and never come out.

**Beats**
1. Infiltrate the monastery disguised as a pilgrim. Don't sing the hymn at the gate, or you lose control of your character briefly.
2. Meet **Sister Anwe**, the only monk still with her own face.
3. **Set piece:** the Hymn Hall, a rhythm-based stealth room where you move only on the beat.
4. Discover that the Choir's leader was a lonely abbot who used the shard of Belonging to make sure nobody would ever be alone.
5. Nim begins to sing with them and remembers her name from before.

**Boss: the Choir of Tallow**
- Phase 1: the body attacks in unison with sound waves.
- Phase 2: shine light on individual faces to separate monks from the mass (each one separated weakens the Choir).
- Phase 3: Anwe sings a counter-melody you must protect. The Choir breaks apart into confused, living monks (Mercy) or burns (Order).

**Lore reveal:** Nim is not human. The Choir recognises her as "the last light".
**Side quests:** *Unsung* (find 5 monks who escaped and hid), *The Bell Without a Clapper* (connects back to Bellhold).

---

## Chapter 6: The Gilded Regent (Solace) · 60 min · Lv 24–30

**Hook:** The capital holds the largest shard and rules with rationed daylight. Bright upper districts live on stolen light while the lower city starves in the dark.

**Beats**
1. Two districts: the dark lower city (rebels, stealth) and the gilded upper city (disguises, dialogue).
2. Meet **Lady Seraphine**, the Regent's daughter, who secretly runs the rebellion.
3. **Wren's confession:** he once broke rebel lanterns for the Regent. Forgive him or exile him.
4. **Set piece, the assault:** your caravan allies (Odile's soldiers, Ysolde's healers, Brannoc's pup, the Choir monks) help depending on who you saved. **Every earlier choice pays off here.**
5. Wren dies holding the gate if he was exiled, or survives if forgiven and Odile is present.
6. The Regent reveals the truth: the sun was dying, and Aurel bound it to save the world. Rationing isn't greed but triage.

**Boss: the Gilded Regent**
- Phase 1: mirror panels redirect beams of light across the arena.
- Phase 2: he steals your light radius, so you fight in the dark.
- Phase 3: turn his mirrors against him. Choose: execute him (Order), let Seraphine judge him (Mercy) or let him descend into the Hollow to see what he has denied (Memory).

**Lore reveal:** under the palace lies a door into the Hollow, and the voice of King Aurel is coming from it.
**Side quests:** *Light Tax* (expose corrupt collectors), *Mirrors of the Upper City* (redirect light to the lower city permanently; visibly changes the map).

---

## Chapter 7: Beneath (The Hollow) · 40 min · Lv 30–35

**Hook:** You descend into the Hollow itself to reach the crown's original location.

**Beats**
1. A dreamlike region where memories from every chapter replay. You walk through scenes of Ysolde's children, Halvard's first night awake, Ines and Tobin as children.
2. Find **Tobin Marrow** alive but faded. Bring him back (Memory +) or let him stay where he is at peace.
3. **The Nim reveal:** she is the last memory of Orrin's daylight, given a body by the Hollow so that someone would remember the sun as it was.
4. Fight mirror versions of all six classes, the "paths not taken".
5. Every Hollow Memory collected so far is visible here as a star.

**Boss: the Hollow Mother**
- She doesn't attack directly. The arena is total darkness, lit only by your flame and Nim.
- Survive waves of grief-shades while protecting Nim.
- If you have 20+ Hollow Memories, a dialogue option ends the fight peacefully.

**Lore reveal:** the Hollow is Orrin's grief. Aurel didn't save the sun; he refused to let it die and has kept it in agony for 300 years.

---

## Finale: The Unlit King (Inside the Crown) · 25 min · Lv 35

**Hook:** Through the Hollow, you enter the crown itself, where Aurel rules on a throne of chained sunlight.

**Beats**
1. Aurel tries to persuade you: take the crown, keep the world lit, become the next king.
2. Your allies appear as light-echoes based on who you saved.
3. **Final fight** with phases that adapt to your choices:
   - High Order: Aurel fights to protect order, using Warden powers you claimed.
   - High Mercy: Aurel begs. The fight is shorter and sadder.
   - High Memory: fought inside replayed memories of the 300 years.
4. The ending choice.

### Endings

| Ending | Requirements | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reforge** | Order is the highest meter and at least 4 shards claimed | You become the new king. Light returns to all cities equally, but you are bound forever. Bittersweet "good king" ending. |
| **Release** | Mercy is the highest meter and Nim survived | Orrin dies peacefully. The world goes dark, then the stars appear for the first time in 300 years. Kindled flames light the world. Hopeful ending. |
| **Join the Hollow** | Memory is the highest meter | Everything is remembered and nothing changes. The world becomes a dream. Strange, beautiful, divisive. |
| **True Hollow** *(secret)* | All 24 Hollow Memories plus the peaceful Hollow Mother outcome | Orrin's grief is healed. A new, small sun is born from Nim. Best ending. |

The epilogue is a slideshow of every region and NPC, reflecting your choices.

## Content totals (for the build plan)

- **Regions:** 9 (prologue, 7 chapters, finale) plus the Cinderwell hub
- **Bosses:** 8 + 1 mini-boss, each with 3 phases and a choice
- **Main quests:** about 40 steps; **side quests:** 18 (2 per region)
- **Collectibles:** 24 Hollow Memories
- **Companions/NPCs with arcs:** 8
- **Enemy types:** about 32 (4 per region)
- **Endings:** 3 + 1 secret

## Measuring the 5-hour target

Main-path playtime is checked each milestone with an in-game timer that logs per-chapter time to the console. If a chapter tests more than 20% short, extend the set piece or add a main-path side quest rather than padding with combat.
